Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Who we are

Mealprint is a tool for calculating and comparing the carbon footprint of recipes. It is operated as a personal project. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@mealprint.app.

What we collect

We collect the minimum necessary to operate the site.

  • Recipe data you submit — recipe names, ingredient lists, and source URLs entered through the Add Recipe form. This data is stored and may be published publicly on the site.
  • Session cookies — temporary cookies required for security (CSRF protection) and for keeping you logged in to the admin area. These expire when your browser session ends or after 1 hour of inactivity.
  • Server logs — standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) retained for up to 30 days for debugging and abuse prevention.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or any account information from public users. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

How we use your data

  • Recipe submissions are displayed publicly as part of the Mealprint recipe index.
  • Session data is used solely to protect forms from CSRF attacks and to maintain admin authentication.
  • Server logs are used only for debugging errors and detecting abuse — never for profiling or advertising.

Data storage and processors

Recipe data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase (infrastructure located in the EU). The web application runs on Render (US-based). Both providers process data on our behalf under their respective data processing agreements.

CO2 emission factors are sourced from third-party climate databases — The Big Climate Database and Agribalyse — and are imported into Mealprint's own database. We do not transmit your data to these sources.

Data retention

  • Published recipes — retained indefinitely as public content unless you request deletion.
  • Session cookies — expire after 1 hour of inactivity or when your browser session ends.
  • Server logs — deleted after 30 days.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing or request restriction
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@mealprint.app.

Cookies

We use session cookies only. These are strictly necessary for the site to function securely (CSRF protection, admin login). We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. No consent banner is required for strictly necessary cookies under EU law, but we disclose them here for transparency.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
