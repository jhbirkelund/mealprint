About Mealprint

Mealprint is a free tool that estimates the carbon footprint of recipes using publicly available climate data. By using this site, you agree to these terms.

Data sources

CO2 emission factors used in Mealprint's calculations come from two third-party lifecycle assessment databases:

  • The Big Climate Database — a Danish database of CO2 and nutritional data for food products, developed by a coalition of Danish food and environmental organisations. Covers approximately 500 ingredients with CO2 and nutrition data.
  • Agribalyse — a French national lifecycle assessment database developed by ADEME (the French Agency for Ecological Transition). Covers approximately 2,500 ingredients with CO2 data.

These databases are imported into Mealprint periodically. The data reflects conditions at the time of import and may not reflect the latest published versions.

CO2 data accuracy

Carbon footprint estimates on Mealprint are informational only. They are based on lifecycle assessment data from the sources above and involve simplifications and approximations.

  • Estimates do not account for regional variation, farming practices, transport distances, or seasonal differences.
  • Results should not be used as the sole basis for sustainability claims, product labelling, or regulatory compliance without independent verification.
  • We make no warranty that the data is accurate, complete, or current.
